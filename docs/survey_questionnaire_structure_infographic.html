Appendix B · Research Instrumentation & Evaluation Framework

Survey Questionnaire Structure (54 Items Total)

Comprehensive academic evaluation instrument distributed to the Supreme Student Government (SSG) officers and recognized student campus organizations at Cebu Technological University – Daanbantayan Campus, assessing pre-implementation operational friction and post-implementation system usability.

Academic Methodology: Survey Instrumentation & Evaluation Design

To ensure empirical rigor in evaluating the SSG Office Assistant Application at Cebu Technological University – Daanbantayan Campus, a structured 54-item evaluation instrument was formulated. The instrument is strategically partitioned into three core segments designed to capture institutional context, establish baseline operational friction, and measure post-intervention usability.

Instrument Scale
54 Items Total
3 Core Parts assessing pre- and post-deployment metrics
Usability Standard
SUS (10 Items)
Standardized System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) benchmark

5-Point Likert

1. Partition Breakdown of the Survey Instrument

  • Part I: Respondent Demographic & Institutional Profile: Captures governance branch (Executive, Legislative, Committees), organizational affiliation (SSG Council or one of the 7 recognized departmental organizations: CODETECH, AQUATECH, FSTLP, THRED, IMPROVERS, EHMA, ACETechs), and office duty frequency to control for operational exposure.
  • Part II: Current System Challenges (20 Items across 5 Categories): Establishes the empirical baseline of pre-implementation bottlenecks. Each category contains 4 targeted Likert items evaluating:
    • Category A: Record Management & Data Integrity (fragmentation of paper records, manual transcription vulnerabilities).
    • Category B: Document Retrieval & Accessibility (retrieval times exceeding 5–10 minutes, unindexed cloud folders).
    • Category C: Financial Tracking & Reconciliation (Death Aid remittance bottlenecks, audit delays of 2–3 days).
    • Category D: Information Dissemination (ineffective group chats, lack of read receipts, communication silos).
    • Category E: Inventory & Resource Monitoring (unrecorded equipment borrowings, uniform stock discrepancies).
  • Part III: System Evaluation & Usability (34 Items across 4 Categories): Evaluates the impact of the centralized digital assistant following deployment:
    • Section 1 (8 Items): Operational Speed & Efficiency (sub-second queries, automated liquidation exports).
    • Section 2 (8 Items): Error Prevention & Data Accuracy (form validation, GridFS checksums, Google Sheets audit sync).
    • Section 3 (10 Items): System Usability Scale (SUS) (Brooke, 1996 standard alternating positive/negative Likert items).
    • Section 4 (8 Items): Global Satisfaction & Institutional Impact (workload reduction, transparency, adoption willingness).

2. Statistical Treatment & Scoring Interpretation

The responses collected through Part II and Part III are scored using a standard 5-point Likert scale. Items in Section 3 (SUS) are converted using Brooke’s standard normalization formula:

  • For odd-numbered items (positive polarity): Score Contribution = Response − 1
  • For even-numbered items (negative polarity): Score Contribution = 5 − Response
  • Total SUS Score is calculated as: SUS = (Sum of Contributions) × 2.5, yielding an industry-standard percentile score out of 100 (where scores above 68 indicate above-average usability, and scores exceeding 80 denote excellent institutional acceptability).
