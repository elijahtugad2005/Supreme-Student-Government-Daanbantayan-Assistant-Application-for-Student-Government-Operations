Chapter 2 / Requirements Specifications: Narrative Interpretation

The Ishikawa (Cause-and-Effect) Fishbone Diagram visualizes the multi-factorial operational bottlenecks observed within the Supreme Student Government (SSG) Office at Cebu Technological University – Daanbantayan Campus. Rather than isolated errors, administrative service delays stem from systemic failures across five interconnected dimensions: People, Processes, Technology, Documents, and Communication.

1. Dimension-by-Dimension Root-Cause Breakdown

  • Technology (Primary Anchor Cause): The overarching vulnerability is the complete absence of a unified system of record. Officers currently rely on an uncoordinated patchwork of tools (Facebook Messenger, local Excel files, unindexed Google Drive folders), resulting in zero automated input validation, no audit trail, and no centralized access control.
  • Documents: Fragmented storage across paper binders and disjointed personal drives produces severe retrieval latencies (5 to 10 minutes per record) and an error rate of approximately 10 duplicates/discrepancies per 100 entries.
  • Processes: Operations follow sequential physical routing where approvals depend on physical signatures. Simple inquiries and financial liquidations stretch into 2- to 3-day delays because audit status cannot be tracked in real time.
  • People: High annual officer turnover without standardized training leads to inconsistent document naming, duplicate record entries, and resistance to shifting away from informal habits.
  • Communication: Crucial campus updates and announcements are lost within personal group chats, leaving campus advisers and department governors uninformed regarding fiscal and operational decisions.

2. Architectural Justification: The Proposed Solution

These identified root causes directly validate the architectural mandate for the SSG Office Assistant Application:

  • Centralized Cloud Architecture (React SPA + Firebase/Firestore + MongoDB GridFS): Replaces fragmented spreadsheets with transactional ACID/NoSQL collections and binary GridFS stores, collapsing document retrieval times from 10 minutes to sub-second indexed queries.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Directly addresses the "People" and "Technology" deficiencies by implementing strict permission boundaries (Admin, Finance Secretary, Secretary, Senator, Representative), eliminating unauthorized access and self-approval loopholes.
  • Automated Broadcast Pipeline (Telegram Bot Webhooks + Live Notification Feeds): Resolves the "Communication" bottleneck by replacing informal Messenger threads with tamper-resistant, verifiable official broadcasts accompanied by read receipts (inline acknowledgment callbacks).
  • Immutable Audit Trail & Financial Ledger: Eliminates the 2- to 3-day turnaround by automating Death Aid remittance matching, order status tracking, and Google Sheets bidirectional audit synchronizations.
