Chapter 3 / Implementation Analysis: Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

During the engineering lifecycle of the SSG Office Assistant Application at Cebu Technological University – Daanbantayan Campus, an external platform barrier threatened the delivery schedule between Week 4 and Week 6 (September 2026). This case study analyzes the technical constraints that prompted a strategic platform pivot from the Meta Facebook Messenger API to the Telegram Bot API.

1. Technical and Institutional Bottlenecks in Facebook Messenger API

  • The Business Verification Deadlock: Meta enforces mandatory Business Verification for applications requesting permissions beyond basic development mode (e.g., pages_messaging). The verification pipeline takes 14 to 30 business days, an unacceptable duration in an academic development sprint where the total testing window was limited to 8 weeks.
  • Paperwork and Legal Entity Incompatibility: Meta requires legal articles of incorporation, corporate utility bills, or commercial licenses in the applicant's exact legal name. As the Supreme Student Government is an autonomous student leadership body within a state university (rather than an incorporated corporate enterprise), securing university board approvals for a third-party developer portal created insurmountable bureaucratic friction.
  • Development Sandbox Restriction: Unverified Meta applications are locked into sandbox mode, allowing interactions only with registered developer accounts. This limitation rendered live field testing, student government announcements, and the distribution of the 54-item evaluation instrument completely impossible.

2. The Telegram Migration Solution: Technical Advantages

  • Rapid Provisioning via @BotFather: The Telegram Bot API requires zero business verification. A production bot token was provisioned in under 5 minutes and integrated into the React SPA and Express middleware in under 6 hours.
  • Friction-Free Public Deployment: Telegram channels allow open subscription models with no developer whitelisting. The SSG secretariat was immediately empowered to broadcast real-time announcements to the university community.
  • Interactive Read-Receipt Verification: Telegram natively supports HTML message formatting and inline keyboard callbacks (callback_data), enabling verifiable read receipts ("❤️ Seen / Acknowledged") directly connected to the campus server.

3. Strategic Decision Justification (ADR Committed: September 28, 2026)

On September 28, 2026, the project team exercised an agile architectural pivot. Rather than halting development to await corporate validation from Meta, communication webhooks were migrated to the Telegram Bot API. This decision eliminated an estimated 18+ days of administrative backlog, kept the engineering milestone on schedule, and ensured the uninterrupted rollout of the 54-item usability survey across all seven recognized departmental campus organizations (CODETECH, AQUATECH, FSTLP, THRED, IMPROVERS, EHMA, and ACETechs).
