# SSG Office Assistant - Domain Glossary

Context: SSG Office Assistant Treasury & Financial Governance Module

## Language

**Fund Account**:
An identifiable repository of institutional money with distinct source classification, holding location, custody, and restrictions.
_Avoid_: Bank account, fund bucket

**Fiscal Year Budget**:
The official annual budget appropriated for the active SSG administration, strictly requiring approved budget resolutions and itemized line items.
_Avoid_: General budget, pocket cash

**FSG Remittance**:
Supplemental funding received from the Federation of Student Governments, tracked as backup allocations for approved activities.
_Avoid_: Federation grant, external pocket

**Income Generated Funds (IGF)**:
General revenue generated from SSG activities (merchandise, pre-orders, uniform sales) supporting flexible officer preparation consumables and student pre-order procurement.
_Avoid_: Revolving fund, petty cash

**Income-Generating Projects (IGP)**:
Self-sustaining revenue-generating projects (e.g. Piso Water Vendo Machine, Locker Rentals) maintained via isolated subledgers.
_Avoid_: Sidelines, side business

**Budget Allocation**:
An authorized earmark of funds designated for a specific approved activity, event, or resolution, carrying itemized line items.
_Avoid_: Reservation, budget earmark

**Financial Transaction**:
An immutable posted ledger entry (receipt, expense, collection, refund, transfer, or reversal) that affects the balance of a Fund Account.
_Avoid_: Log entry, budget row

**Reversal**:
An authorized compensating transaction linked to a previously posted entry to negate its financial effect without physically deleting history.
_Avoid_: Void, delete transaction

**Pre-Order Batch**:
A lifecycle-managed procurement run for student merchandise requiring quote approval, collection tracking, dual-officer supplier payment approval, and audit clearance.
_Avoid_: Product campaign, order group

**Audit Clearance**:
A verified status reached by a Pre-Order Batch once all student payments, supplier receipts, and fulfillment claims are reconciled by the Audit Secretary.
_Avoid_: Audit sign-off, closing check
